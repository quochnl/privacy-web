Clock Face: Standby & Widgets

Official Privacy Policy for the Clock Face: Standby & Widgets iOS Application.

Overview & Commitment

Your privacy is our utmost priority. Clock Face: Standby & Widgets is designed from the ground up to respect your personal information. The app does not require account registration, does not collect personal contact details, and operates primarily offline on your device.

Data Storage & Local Settings

All your clock preferences, custom DIY watch configurations, alarm times, focus timer presets, and soundscape selections are stored exclusively on your local device via iOS UserDefaults and local sandboxed storage. We do not transmit or store your clock customizations on remote servers.

Widgets & StandBy Mode

When you use our iOS 17+ Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets or utilize the StandBy bedside clock mode, data is rendered natively by Apple's WidgetKit framework entirely on your device. No device telemetry or location data is gathered during these operations.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Any premium subscriptions or one-time purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store In-App Purchase infrastructure (StoreKit). We never receive or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

Analytics & Diagnostic Data

To ensure app stability and fix unforeseen crashes, the app may collect anonymous diagnostic information (such as crash stack traces and device model) via standard platform APIs. This data is strictly aggregated and contains no personally identifiable information (PII).

Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for general audiences and complies with children's privacy regulations including COPPA. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.
